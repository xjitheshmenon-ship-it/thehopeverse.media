**3. Longevity, the one-number story: 146–194 years (US, EU, India)**
- **Why it matters:** A study in npj Aging modelled life with every reversible hallmark of aging removed except somatic mutations. It estimated a median lifespan of 146 to 194 years. Healthline covered it on 1 Aug 2026.
- **Rare detail:** Neurons and heart muscle cells, which can't divide, are the bottlenecks. The cap comes from the cells you can't replace.
- **Interest signal:** Longevity-related web searches quadrupled in a year. (Genetic Literacy Project, 21 Oct 2026)

- **Fits the niche:** Yes (one-number "future" story).
