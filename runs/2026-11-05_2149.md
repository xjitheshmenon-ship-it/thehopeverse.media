## Caveats to say on air
- **Intent:** We can't know why Moko did it. The Dolphin Communication Project says the whales may simply have followed whatever swam out to sea. It also says the animals probably did not exchange complex information. The only account is from the human witnesses.
- **Rescue vs coincidence:** The "rescue" label is the witnesses' interpretation. The whales' behaviour did change when Moko arrived. Smith said so himself.
- **Pronoun:** Early reports say "she". Moko was later confirmed male (DOC, RNZ). Use "il", and don't repeat the 2008 "she".
- **Season:** Time says "summer of 2008". It was March, which is autumn in NZ, so Time is wrong on that detail.
- **Moko's reputation:** He was not purely a gentle hero. In July 2009 he kept a swimmer from returning to shore, and she said he meant no harm (BBC, via Wikipedia). Avoid a pure "angel" framing.
