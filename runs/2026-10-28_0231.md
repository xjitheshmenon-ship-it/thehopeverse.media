## Why a claimed strike moves fuel prices

1. **Risk premium.** Traders price in the chance that supply is lost, not only actual losses. A claim about the world's top crude exporter can lift prices even if the damage is small.
2. **Futures.** Oil is traded on futures contracts, which are bets on future price. Algorithms react to headlines within seconds. Prices can then fall back if a Saudi denial or confirmation of no damage follows.
