## 1. Setup
- ✅ **Bees:** RMIT PhD researcher Scarlett Howard marked individual honeybees for identification and lured them to a purpose-built test apparatus. The bees were trained to choose the image with the lowest number of elements to get a sugar-solution reward.
- ✅ **Training examples:** the bees learned to choose three elements over four, or two over three.
- ✅ **Empty card test:** when tested with a blank image against an image with one or more elements, the bees treated zero as the lower number, despite never having been exposed to an "empty set".
- ✅ **Test conditions:** the test trials were run without rewards. The paper's own phrasing is that bees showed an understanding that zero lies at the lower end of the numerical continuum.
- ✅ **Scope:** the work was done in both Australia and France, with many control experiments. The shapes were dark elements on cards.
