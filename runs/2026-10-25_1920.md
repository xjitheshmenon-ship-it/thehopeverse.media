### How listing works in plain words
1. The company sets a price range, and investors bid within it.
2. Institutions bid first, as anchors, and are locked in for a period.
3. After the issue closes, the final price is set. The brokers' pages here show the issue price only as the band.
4. If there are more bids than shares, allotment is by lottery for retail. Unsuccessful bids get their money back.
5. On listing day the shares start trading on NSE and BSE, and the price is then set by buyers and sellers. It can open above or below the issue price.
