## Coalition math (from memory, unverified in this search)

I'd check these against the Basic Law: The Government and the Knesset site, and a second source, before publishing.
- **Majority:** 61 of 120.
- **President's role:** After the results are certified, the president consults party leaders and tasks the MK with the best chance of forming a government. That is not automatically the leader of the largest party.
- **Timeline:** The nominee gets 28 days, and the president can extend it by up to 14 days.
- **If the nominee fails:** The president can task another MK, with 28 days. Then the Knesset has 21 days to back a candidate with 61 signatures.
- **Deadlock:** If that fails, the Knesset dissolves automatically and a new election is held, normally within about 90 days.
