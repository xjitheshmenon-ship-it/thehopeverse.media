## What the whale did, with quotes
These quotes are from the Chronicle reprint.
- She floated passively and "giving off a strange kind of vibration".
- Moskito: "When I was cutting the line going through the mouth, its eye was there winking at me, watching me… It was an epic moment of my life."
- Once free she swam in circles. Moskito said she swam to each diver, nuzzled him, then moved to the next.
- Moskito: "It felt to me like it was thanking us, knowing that it was free and that we had helped it."
- Moskito: "It seemed kind of affectionate, like a dog that's happy to see you… I never felt threatened."
- Another quote from the Chronicle: "It stopped about a foot away from me, pushed me around a little bit and had some fun." (I saw this in the Gans post. Check it against the full article.)

## The caveat for the script
The caveat is in the Chronicle itself: whale experts say it's nice to think she was thanking them, but nobody really knows what was on her mind. Menigoz said: "I don't know for sure what it was thinking." Stoudt noted that humpbacks generally "don't like to be interacted with".

**Suggested wording:** "The divers felt she was thanking them. Scientists say we can't know."

**Risk to mention:** Stoudt said a flip of a humpback's tail can kill a person. This was dangerous and is not a template for amateur rescue. Entangled whales should be reported to trained teams.
