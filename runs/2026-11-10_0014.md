## What scientists said (confirmed)

- **John Bradshaw** (University of Bristol, anthrozoologist): speculated Holly might have got a lift, perhaps sneaking under the hood of a truck heading down I-95, but her paws suggest she was not driven all the way. A second page quotes him saying cats can sense smells from far away.

## The fact most coverage missed, and the honest caveat

The NYT piece says that two weeks later, an animal-rescue worker called the Richters to say a cat resembling Holly had been seen eating behind the Daytona Hooters, where employees put out food for feral cats. So she was probably still near Daytona at that point. That leaves about six weeks for the walk home.

This fits an honest caveat. Her worn paws and heavy weight loss (WLRN/NPR commentator Barbara King wrote she lost nearly half her body weight, one source only, so use neutral wording) suggest she walked a long way.
