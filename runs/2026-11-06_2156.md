## Honest ending and scientific caveat
- **Ending:** Elsa died young, at about five, of a tick-borne disease, and not from any hunt. Keep the tone factual and calm.
- **Caveat on the cubs:** habituated lions can become a problem. The cubs attacked goats belonging to local herders. Serengeti's authorities then worried about tourist safety near lions used to humans. They made the Adamsons leave, even though the Adamsons feared the cubs would starve. The Adamsons never saw the cubs together again. George found Little Elsa later, healthy and with two unrelated lions.
- **Broader scientific critique of hand-rearing and release:** I have not read a source on this. Possible sources are Born Free Foundation statements and academic papers on lion rehabilitation. Do not state a general "science says" claim until you have one.
