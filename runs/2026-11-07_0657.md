## Is "il devient celui qui rassure les autres" fair?

It is partly fair, but it isn't a scientific finding. Treat it as the caregivers' interpretation.

- **What is documented:** Robinson, who was there, was puzzled that Jasper stayed calm on arrival when the other bears reacted with anger. She wrote that his calm was a mystery. The staff say he later greeted new arrivals and interrupted fights.
- **What isn't documented:** Bekoff's line that Jasper tells other bears "All will be okay, trust me" is his gloss, not a quote from Jasper. I found no behavioural study, no recorded count of how many bears he greeted, and no independent observer. The "ambassador for forgiveness" label is a human, campaign-friendly framing. Animals Asia also used Jasper as the face of its anti-bile-farming campaign.
- **Honest wording for the Short:** "Selon ses soigneurs, Jasper accueillait les nouveaux arrivants et calmait les disputes." Or: "Ses soigneurs disent qu'il rassurait les autres. Personne ne peut savoir ce qu'il ressentait, mais le comportement a été observé pendant des années."
