# Save the Date: outline (written before the music)

**Use:** a 1-minute wedding announcement video for a modern couple. Grand, cinematic intro and climax.
**Story:** a young couple in today's city: how they met, their moments together, then the big reveal of the wedding date.
**Feeling:** wonder and warmth at the start, joy building, a huge proud celebration at the date reveal.

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 s | Black screen, city lights blur into focus. | Shimmering strings and celesta; a soft wordless female voice hums the love tune. |
| 4-9 s | Two names appear in gold. | Timpani roll and the full orchestra swells into one grand brass and choir fanfare hit. |
| 9-22 s | Candid moments: coffee, laughter, a rainy walk. | The same tune on felt piano with the humming voice, a light modern groove, warm bass. |
| 22-36 s | The proposal on a rooftop at sunset. | Strings carry the tune, tabla and dhol groove grow, flute answers each phrase. |
| 36-52 s | Friends and family cheer; confetti; the couple spin. | Climax: the tune a step higher, full orchestra, choir, big drums, humming soaring on top. |
| 52-60 s | "SAVE THE DATE" and the date. | Three rising full-band hits, a final grand chord, and the humming voice sings the first phrase once more. |

**Raga:** Hamsadhwani in D (bright, auspicious, the classic sound of a happy beginning). One tune all the way through, one continuous generation.
