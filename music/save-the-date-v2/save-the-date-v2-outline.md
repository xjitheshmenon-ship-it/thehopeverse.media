# Save the Date v2: outline (written before the music)

**Feedback on v1 (30 Oct 13:03):** "Too basic. Make it interesting and attention making."
**Idea:** the whole film is a countdown to the wedding day. Time itself is the hook: a ticking clock, heartbeats and camera clicks play the rhythm, and the wedding drum-and-horn burst (kettimelam) explodes at the climax as a modern hybrid score.
**Feeling:** cheeky and catchy from second one, surprises every 8-10 s, a massive celebration at the date reveal.

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Black. A clock ticks; a heartbeat thumps. A camera shutter clicks. | Ticks and heartbeat are the beat; a bold whistled 4-note hook cuts in. |
| 3-8 s | Flash cuts of the couple, in sync with the clicks. | Finger snaps, punchy 808, plucked synth; the hook on whistle, a female voice answers "aa-ha". |
| 8-10 s | Everything freezes mid-laugh. | Sudden full stop: one tick in silence. |
| 10-24 s | Their story in quick, fun cuts. | Slam back in: groove with tabla and 808, strings stab the hook, the hook passes to bansuri. |
| 24-34 s | He kneels on the rooftop; she gasps. | Build: orchestra rises, the female voice hums the hook high, a reversed swell sucks in. |
| 34-36 s | Ring on the finger, the screen goes black. | A huge hit, then silence with a single heartbeat. |
| 36-52 s | Family, friends, confetti, dancing. | Explosion: thavil drums and nadaswaram-style horn frenzy with big cinematic drums, full orchestra and choir, the hook at its biggest, a key lift at 44 s. |
| 52-60 s | "SAVE THE DATE" and the date, ticking into place. | The clock ticks back, three slamming hits on the date, the whistle plays the hook one last time over a final boom. |

**Raga:** Mohanam in D (bright, playful, can't sound sad). One hook all the way through, one continuous generation.
