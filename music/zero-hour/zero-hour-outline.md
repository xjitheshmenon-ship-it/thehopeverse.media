# Zero Hour: outline (written before the music)

**Genre:** modern big-screen action thriller, main title theme. About 90 s, hype from the first second.
**Story:** Mumbai, today. At midnight a cyber attack blacks out the whole city. One young undercover agent has 60 minutes to reach the control tower and switch the city back on, chased across rooftops, highways and the sea link.
**Feeling:** pressure, speed and swagger from the start, rising to a huge victory when the lights return.

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | The skyline goes dark in one wave, block by block. | One huge low boom and a brass blast, then the 3-note call on a gritty synth. |
| 3-14 s | Red emergency lights. The agent's eyes open in the dark. | Ticking clock pulse, deep synth bass, low male choir, strings pumping fast. |
| 14-26 s | She sprints across rooftops, a drone behind her. | Hybrid drums and a real thavil kick in; the main hook on synth lead and brass together. |
| 26-38 s | Bike chase down the dark highway, sparks and headlights. | Hook passes to a fiery solo violin with slides; driving strings; choir shouts on the accents. |
| 38-50 s | Freeway pile-up; she flips over the car and keeps running. | Bigger: brass and choir carry the hook, the drums double up. One beat of total silence, then a slam. |
| 50-64 s | Face-off with the hacker in the control tower. | A bansuri stabs the hook in fast runs, answered by the synth lead; tension rises. |
| 64-80 s | She pulls the switch. The city lights up again, wave by wave. | The hook a whole step higher, full orchestra, choir and drums, the biggest moment. |
| 80-90 s | She walks out into the bright city. Title: ZERO HOUR. | Three rising hits, a final boom, and the 3-note call on brass. |

**Raga:** Shanmukhapriya in E (dark, fierce, with a sharp edge). One hook all the way through, one continuous generation.
