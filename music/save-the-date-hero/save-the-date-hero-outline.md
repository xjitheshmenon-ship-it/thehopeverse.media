# Save the Date: Hero Walk-in (outline, written before the music)

**Ask (30 Oct 16:45):** "a stylish smooth save the date, who look like a hero in a movie."
**Idea:** the couple's entry shot from a big-screen action film. Slow motion, sunglasses, wind in the coat, the city behind them. Cool, confident and smooth, not loud for the sake of it. Built on the swagger of Dark Horse, which he liked, but smoother and warmer for a wedding.

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 s | Black. A lighter flicks; boots on wet tarmac. | One solo electric guitar, clean and smoky, plays the hook alone with slow bends. |
| 4-8 s | Two silhouettes walk out of the light in slow motion. | Deep 808 sub swells in, a finger snap and a low male hum join; tension rises. |
| 8 s | They lift their sunglasses. | One heavy, smooth slam: the full groove drops in. |
| 8-24 s | Slow-motion walk through the city, coat tails flying. | Laid-back swagger groove, deep sub, crisp snaps, the hook on a smooth synth lead with bends; a cool whistle answers. |
| 24-36 s | He offers his hand; she takes it with a smirk. | A soft female voice hums the hook over the groove; strings glide in underneath. |
| 36-37 s | Freeze frame. | A half-second stop. |
| 37-52 s | They turn to camera together, sparks and lights behind them. | The peak: the hook an octave higher on lead and strings, deep male chant answering, big but smooth drums. |
| 52-60 s | "SAVE THE DATE" and the date burn onto the screen. | Three cool full-band hits and the solo guitar plays the hook one last time over a deep boom that rings out. |

**Raga:** Kiravani in E (E minor with a raised 7th, the classic stylish hero colour). One key, one tempo, one hook, one continuous generation. No key change (v2's key lift broke the flow).
