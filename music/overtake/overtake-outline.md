# Overtake: outline (written before the music)

**Genre:** modern global action film, car chase cue. 60 s, hype from the first second, for a worldwide audience.
**Story:** A rain-soaked megacity at night, today. A young getaway driver has a stolen hard drive and five minutes to cross the city. Police cars and a drone chase her through traffic, a tunnel and a closing bridge. She shifts up a gear every time they get close.
**Idea that makes it ours:** the track "shifts gears". Every ~10 s a new gear: a new lead sound takes the hook and the drive gets harder. One hard brake in the middle (the near miss), then she floors it.
**Feeling:** speed, swagger and nerve from 0 s; a near miss; a huge escape.
**Learned from Red Line (car chase v1-v3):** keep the hard brake and a surprise; no engine growl (he disliked it); a new lead every 8-12 s (prompt mode with a timeline does this, plan mode ignored timed stops).

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Headlights snap on in the dark garage. | One huge low hit, then the 3-note call (D F A, last note bent up) on a gritty synth. |
| 3-12 s | Gear 1: she tears out into the rain. | Fast string ostinato and a tight hybrid drum groove at full speed at once; the hook on the synth lead. |
| 12-22 s | Gear 2: sirens close in, weaving through traffic. | The hook moves to a distorted electric guitar riff with bends; brass stabs hit with it. |
| 22-32 s | Gear 3: the drone drops down over the highway. | The hook moves to a fiery solo violin with slides; taiko-style drums double up. |
| 32-36 s | THE HARD BRAKE: a truck swings across, she stops inches away. | The whole band brakes on one falling brass-and-strings slide, one beat of silence, a ticking pulse alone. |
| 36-48 s | Gear 4: she floors it through the tunnel. | Full band slams back, the hook a whole step higher on brass and synth together, crowd shouts "hey". |
| 48-60 s | Gear 5: she jumps the closing bridge and is gone. | The biggest section, everything trading the hook; three rising hits, final boom, the 3-note call alone. |

**Raga:** Kiravani in D (D E F G A Bb C#): dark, driving, familiar to world ears but bends like South Indian film music. One hook all the way, one continuous generation, 2 takes; Mr. Raga picks the better one.
