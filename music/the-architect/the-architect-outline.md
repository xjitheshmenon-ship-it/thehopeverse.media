# The Architect: outline (written before the music)

**Genre:** global big-screen thriller, the villain's theme BGM. About 90 s. Made for a worldwide audience: today's world, no country-specific setting.
**Villain:** a calm, smiling tech billionaire who quietly owns every screen on Earth. He never shouts. He doesn't need to.
**Feeling:** cold elegance and swagger from the first second, growing into crushing power. Dark, stylish, unstoppable.

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 s | Black screen. One fingertip taps on glass. Every screen on Earth flickers. | A cold, close whistle plays the villain hook alone; a fingertip tap on glass is the click. |
| 4-8 s | His face, lit blue by a wall of screens. A slow smile. | Dry low piano and a cello section join under the hook; a deep server-room hum becomes the drone. |
| 8-18 s | New York, London, Tokyo, Dubai, Sao Paulo: every billboard turns to his logo. | Full aura: heavy half-time hybrid drums with swagger, brass blasts the hook, low male choir. |
| 18-30 s | He walks through his glass tower; guards step aside without a word. | The hook on a growling electric cello with slides; string ostinato; keyboard clicks as the hi-hat. |
| 30-34 s | He stops at the window, the city far below. | Drums and bass step out; only the whistle and the piano, like a thought. |
| 34-46 s | He presses one key. Stock markets crash; a whole city goes dark. | Slam: full orchestra and choir, the hook bigger, toms doubling. |
| 46-58 s | The faces of the people who could stop him vanish from the screens one by one. | A sinister solo violin answers the hook in fast runs; a hit on each face erased. |
| 58-60 s | He turns to the camera. | Surprise: everything stops except one tap on glass, then a full-band slam. |
| 60-78 s | He looks straight at us and smiles. | The hook one step higher, full orchestra, choir and drums: the biggest moment. |
| 78-90 s | His reflection in the glass becomes the title: THE ARCHITECT. | Three heavy rising hits, a final boom, the whistle plays the hook's first notes once more, alone. |

**Raga:** Simhendramadhyamam in D (sly and dangerous, a stylish villain's colour). One own hook all the way through, one continuous generation. None of the shared devices from other tracks (no 3-note call, no hey/ho chants, no reversed swell).
