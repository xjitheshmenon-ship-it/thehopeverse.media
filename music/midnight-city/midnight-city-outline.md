# Midnight City: outline (written before the music)

**Genre:** modern city drama, the main theme of a young founder's story. About 90 s.
**Story:** 2 AM in Bengaluru. A young woman's startup is failing, her team has gone home, and she has one night before the investors meet. She rides the empty metro, finds the idea, builds it all night, and walks into the launch at sunrise.
**Feeling:** lonely, restless night that turns into confident, modern triumph.

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-8 s | Empty glass office at 2 AM, one laptop glowing, city lights outside. | A soft synth pulse and the 3-note call on a clean electric piano. |
| 8-20 s | She shuts the laptop and walks out into the neon street. | Soft female humming sings the main melody over a deep 808 hum. |
| 20-34 s | The empty night metro; rain streaks the window; she watches the city go by. | A half-time beat with trap hi-hats and a light tabla; the bansuri answers the humming. |
| 34-46 s | A spark: she sees the idea in the city lights and starts typing on the train. | A synth arpeggio speeds up, a reversed swell, the melody climbs. |
| 46-70 s | All-night building montage: code, coffee, whiteboards, the sky turning blue. | The full drop: the same melody on a bright synth lead, humming and electric guitar together, punchy drums and 808 bass. |
| 70-82 s | Sunrise. She walks into the pitch room and the screen lights up. | The melody comes back a step higher, the biggest moment. |
| 82-90 s | She smiles at the city from the window. | The beat drops away, the humming sings the first phrase once more, the call on electric piano ends it. |

**Raga:** Charukesi in D (bright start, then the flat sixth gives the ache), played with today's sounds. One melody all the way through, one continuous generation.
