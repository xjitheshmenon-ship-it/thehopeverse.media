# ÆTHER Carnatic EDM album: plan (3 Nov 2026)

Jithesh: the first EDM album was "a bit boring, usual music tone", nothing with hype or a high note that grabs attention. Use Carnatic music knowledge in the EDM process.

**The idea:** every track is built around a real Carnatic technique, used as the EDM hype moment instead of a generic drop.

| Device | What it is | How it becomes EDM hype |
|---|---|---|
| Tara sthayi | the singer's high upper octave | a long held high note over every drop: the "high note that grabs attention" |
| Korvai | a phrase played 3 times, landing exactly on beat 1 | the build-up before every drop |
| Sangathi | each repeat of a line sung with more ornament | the hook gets richer every time instead of looping |
| Swara singing | sa ri ga ma pa da ni | the vocal hook (real syllables, not gibberish) |
| Nadai change (tisram) | the beat switches to triplets | a gear-shift surprise in the last drop |
| Tani avartanam | percussion duel: mridangam, ghatam, kanjira, thavil | the breakdown |
| Gamakam | Carnatic shakes and slides | on every lead, so it never sounds like a preset synth |

**Track 1, Tara** (Hamsadhwani, 128 BPM, 2:30): grabs at 0 s with the singer's high note alone, korvai builds, two drops with the held high note, tani breakdown, triplet final drop. Brief tracks/tara-carnatic-edm.json.
Next tracks (after his verdict on Tara): Tisram (nadai changes, Kiravani, drum & bass), Tani (hard techno percussion duel, thavil), Kalpana (fast swara improvisation as the drop lead, Shanmukhapriya, future bass).

**Overtake extended** (2:30): keeps the delivered F1 take for the first 50 s, then new laps: korvai build, drop with the female high note, triplet gear-shift, mridangam vs thavil duel, final lap, korvai ending. Brief tracks/overtake-f1-extended.json.
