# The Long Way Home: outline (written before the music)

**Genre:** emotional family drama (not hero, not royal). Main title theme, about 90 s.
**Story:** a daughter who left her seaside village after a fight with her father comes home ten years later. He is old now and still mends his fishing boat alone.
**Feeling:** bittersweet longing that turns into warm hope.

| Time | Scene | Music |
|---|---|---|
| 0-8 s | Dawn at the sea. The old father mends his boat alone. | Lone flute plays the 3-note call over a soft drone. |
| 8-22 s | Her bus stops at the village. She walks down the sand road. | Soft female humming sings the theme; chopped veena answers in the electronic layer. |
| 22-40 s | Memories: as a child she rode the boat on his shoulders. | Gentle tabla sway joins, warm bass, violins under the humming. |
| 40-50 s | The memory of the fight; she stops at the gate. | Drums thin out, one violin takes the bitter turn of the melody, the humming holds one long note. |
| 50-72 s | He looks up and drops his tools. They hold each other. | Reversed veena swell, then the theme in full: humming, violins and flute together, full groove, lifted a step higher. |
| 72-90 s | Together they push the boat into the sea at sunrise. | The theme softens; the humming sings the first phrase once more; the flute ends on the 3-note call. |

**Raga:** Charukesi in D (bittersweet: bright start, the flat sixth darkens the phrase). One melody all the way through, one continuous generation.
