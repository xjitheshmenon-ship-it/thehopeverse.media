# Four 3-minute ÆTHER songs: outlines (written before the music)

Jithesh, 1 Nov 2026: 3-4 songs, 3 minutes each, inspired by today's EDM list (incl. Classique). Traits only, no names in prompts. Made one at a time once ElevenLabs is back.

## Midnight Metro (nostalgic synthwave, 3 min)
**Story:** Mumbai, 1 AM. A young woman rides the last metro home after a long day; the city lights blur past; she smiles at an old memory. It wasn't a sad story.

**Sound:** Jithesh 1 Nov: 3-4 songs, 3 min, inspired by the EDM list incl. 'Classique' by Adieu Aru (French touch / chillsynth). Traits only, no names in the prompt. Bittersweet, nostalgic, warm. Soft female humming (he loves it). Charukesi in C (major lower half, minor upper half = happy-sad).

**Tempo:** 104 BPM, 3 min. Brief: tracks/midnight-metro.json

## Sunrise Highway (festival anthem, 3 min)
**Story:** Three friends drive out of the city at dawn on an open highway, windows down, singing, as the sun rises over the ghats.

**Sound:** Big-room / progressive house euphoria with a crowd singalong hook (traits from the 2026 festival anthems and the classic singalong anthems). Bright major key, Mohanam in D. No key lift (lesson: ElevenLabs slides through clashing chords).

**Tempo:** 128 BPM, 3 min. Brief: tracks/sunrise-highway.json

## Rooftop Rani (Indian percussion house with a bass flip, 3 min)
**Story:** A Bengaluru rooftop party at night: the dancers move to a hypnotic hand-drum groove until the DJ flips the track into heavy bass and the whole roof jumps.

**Sound:** Afro-house style groove played on Indian hand drums (mridangam, tabla, ghatam, kanjira) + a surprise mid-track flip into heavy bass (traits from the 2026 afro house and tech/bass house hybrids). Hamsadhwani in E keeps it bright. The flip is a style change, not a silence (ElevenLabs ignores short silences).

**Tempo:** 122 BPM, 3 min. Brief: tracks/rooftop-rani.json

## Monsoon Rave (one hook rising to a huge drop, 3 min)
**Story:** The first monsoon storm hits a packed open-air rave; the crowd keeps dancing in the rain as the lights flash with the thunder.

**Sound:** One short phrase repeated and lifted again and again until it explodes, plus a classical-style string melody turned into trance (traits from the classic trance/progressive anthems). Kiravani in A. Rain as part of the rhythm.

**Tempo:** 130 BPM, 3 min. Brief: tracks/monsoon-rave.json
